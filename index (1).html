    Scam playbook

    “Your account will be blocked” (KYC and bank alerts)

    Banks never ask you to update KYC through a link in an SMS, and never ask for your OTP, PIN or CVV. The deadline is there to stop you thinking.

    Parcel held at customs

    A small “redelivery fee” on a lookalike courier site is a card-theft page. Check the order in the courier’s real app instead.

    Lottery, prize and cashback wins

    You cannot win a draw you never entered. Any fee to “release” a prize is the scam itself.

    Work-from-home and task jobs

    Pay for liking videos or completing tasks, often via Telegram, starts small and ends with a deposit you never get back.

    UPI collect requests and QR codes

    You scan a QR to pay, never to receive. If someone says a code will send you money, it will take money.

    Fake tech support and remote access

    No genuine company asks you to install AnyDesk or TeamViewer so they can “fix” your bank account.

    How it works: every link and message is scored by two systems: an explainable rule engine, and machine-learning models (TF-IDF with logistic regression for text, a feature-based logistic regression for links) trained by backend/train.py. The final score blends both. Scores are an aid, not a guarantee. When in doubt, contact the organisation through its official app or number, and report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in (India: call 1930).
